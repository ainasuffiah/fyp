# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Patients are government clinics especially are afected by inefficient queue management and long waiting times.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Most basic queue systems arrange patients based on who arrives first, without fully considering their urgency, waiting time, or appointment status. This can make it difficult to manage different patient priorities and distribute patients fairly across available queues.

## Why does it matter?
Patients may have to wait longer than necessary, while urgent cases may need to be prioritised. Poor queue management can also increase staff workload and make it harder for hospitals to manage their daily operations efficiently.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Managing patient queues in hospitals can be difficult, especially when there are many patients waiting. Normal queue systems may not consider factors like urgency and waiting time. Therefore, this project aims to develop a web-based queue system using weighted priority scheduling to help manage patient queues more efficiently.
